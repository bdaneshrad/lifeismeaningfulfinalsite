The 27 Meaning Drivers

Meaning Drivers are the 27 values that drive meaning in our lives.

They are the reason a moment mattered. I ask people one question: what is the most meaningful moment of your life? Across hundreds of answers, the same 27 values kept showing up.

RelationalSelfTranscendentExpressive

No. 1 · What a Meaning Driver is

it answers one question: why did this matter?

A Meaning Driver is the value that generated the meaning in a moment. When someone tells me the most meaningful moment of their life, the Meaning Drivers are what made it meaningful.

This came out of my research. I asked hundreds of people for the most meaningful moment of their life, studied every answer, and found that the moments kept coming back to the same 27 values. Most moments carry two to four of them at once.

Your most meaningful moments are direct evidence of your own values. Reading them helps you find what drives meaning in your life, as opposed to what you assume does or were told should.

No. 3 · The values inside each one

same Meaning Driver. two different lives.

Inside every Meaning Driver is a smaller set of values. The Meaning Driver tells you where the meaning came from. The values name what, inside it, was yours.

Two people can share a Meaning Driver and have almost nothing in common underneath it. That is why each one below is listed with the values inside it.

Domain 1 of 4 · 9 Meaning Drivers

relational

Meaning that comes through other people. A bond that lasts a lifetime, or a stranger who made you feel seen for a minute. About six in ten moments in the archive touch it.

  • Family

    Moments where family, chosen or given, reveals the depth of its love, shapes who you are, or reminds you what you are living for.

    Values inside itBelonging · Love · Support · Legacy · Sacrifice

  • Love

    The experience of being chosen, held, or loved unconditionally, transforming how you see yourself and reorienting your life around another person through deep emotional connection.

    Values inside itAcceptance · Commitment · Devotion · Intimacy · Surrender · Connection

  • Relationships

    The friendships and bonds, outside of romance and family, that shape who you become, show up when it matters most, and give your life its texture and depth.

    Values inside itTrust · Loyalty · Support · Connection · Authenticity · Friendship

  • Connection

    The felt experience of being seen, understood, or in sync with oneself, another person, group, or moment.

    Values inside itUnderstanding · Presence · Intimacy · Empathy · Belonging · Seen

  • Community

    The meaning found in being part of something larger than yourself: a group, culture, or place where you are known, accepted, and held.

    Values inside itBelonging · Identity · Support · Togetherness · Culture · Unity · Collaboration

  • Parenthood

    Meaning derived from the profound reorientation that comes from being responsible for another life, discovering in that devotion a love deeper than you knew you were capable of and a purpose that extends far beyond yourself.

    Values inside itNurturing · Responsibility · Love · Sacrifice · Devotion · Wonder

  • Kindness

    A discrete, often unexpected gesture of care or generosity that makes someone feel cared for and creates deep emotional impact.

    Values inside itGenerosity · Compassion · Thoughtfulness · Grace

  • Mentorship and Teaching

    Sharing wisdom or guidance that shapes someone else's path, or the impact of being guided by someone who believed in your potential.

    Values inside itGuidance · Wisdom · Impact · Legacy · Belief

  • Service

    Finding meaning through giving your time, energy, or care to others, whether through a single encounter or a lifetime of dedication, and discovering in that giving a deeper sense of who you are and why you are here.

    Values inside itCompassion · Impact · Generosity · Dedication · Humility · Purpose

Domain 2 of 4 · 8 Meaning Drivers

self

Meaning that comes through your relationship with yourself. Who you are, what you're capable of, and where you're going. It shows up in about six in ten moments too.

  • Growth and Discovery

    Meaning driven by a fundamental transformation in identity through self-discovery, emotional development, or expanded self-belief.

    Values inside itAwareness · Transformation · Confidence · Reflection · Learning · Identity

  • Authenticity

    Living, speaking, or acting in alignment with one's true self, often marked by vulnerability and courage expressed in clear, transcendent moments.

    Values inside itHonesty · Vulnerability · Courage · Freedom · Self-Respect · Integrity · Wholeheartedness

  • Resilience and Suffering

    Moving through adversity, trauma, or suffering that cannot always be fixed or escaped, and discovering, whether in the midst of it or on the other side, a strength, meaning, or version of yourself you didn't know existed. Even when suffering can't be removed, meaning can still be chosen in how you carry it.

    Values inside itStrength · Courage · Perseverance · Hope · Independence · Forgiveness · Safety · Acceptance

  • Accomplishment

    The experience of achieving something hard-won, proving to yourself what you are capable of and permanently raising the ceiling of what you believe is possible for your life.

    Values inside itExcellence · Determination · Confidence · Recognition · Mastery · Pride · Self Discipline

  • Health and Well-being

    The moments when your relationship with your body, mind, or health is tested or restored, revealing the fragility of life and creating a deep sense of gratitude and perspective.

    Values inside itHealing · Gratitude · Presence · Balance · Vitality

  • Heritage

    The discovery or reconnection with one's ancestral, cultural, or ethnic origins, creating a felt sense of who you are, where you come from, and what you are meant to carry forward.

    Values inside itIdentity · Legacy · Continuity · Belonging · Home · Tradition

  • Purpose

    The cosmic, energetic moment when the direction of your life suddenly becomes clear, and you feel the profound relief, confidence, energy, and conviction of knowing what you are meant to do.

    Values inside itClarity · Direction · Meaning · Fulfillment · Vision · Intuition

  • Freedom

    The profound meaning found in attaining or reclaiming freedom (from oppression, constraint, or circumstance) and the deep sense of dignity, safety, and self-determination that comes with it.

    Values inside itLiberation · Safety · Autonomy · Dignity · Power

Domain 3 of 4 · 5 Meaning Drivers

transcendent

Meaning that comes from meeting something larger than you. The scale of nature, how short life is, losing someone, the sacred. About four in ten moments touch it.

  • Spirituality and Faith

    The moments of connecting or surrendering to something infinite and larger than yourself, whether through God, faith, nature, or an unseen force, that make you feel protected, guided, and part of a meaning that extends beyond your own life.

    Values inside itTranscendence · Surrender · Peace · Guidance

  • Nature and Awe

    Immersive encounters with natural beauty that evoke wonder, stillness, or a sense of being part of something larger.

    Values inside itWonder · Peace · Connection · Beauty · Presence

  • Grief and Loss

    The experience of losing someone that reveals what we love and value most deeply, reshaping how we live, how we show up for others, and what we refuse to take for granted.

    Values inside itLove · Cherishing · Memory · Honor · Transformation · Patience

  • Time

    The awareness of life's preciousness, whether through the urgency of its finitude or the experience of time standing still in a moment so full that nothing else exists.

    Values inside itPresence · Appreciation · Intentionality · Savoring · Aliveness · Urgency

  • Gratitude and Perspective

    A profound shift in how you see the world or appreciate your life, often triggered by contrast, loss, or a moment of stillness, that leaves you with a deeper understanding of what truly matters and changes how you show up in the world.

    Values inside itAppreciation · Awareness · Humility · Reflection · Simplicity · Presence · Contentment · Peace

Domain 4 of 4 · 5 Meaning Drivers

expressive

Meaning that comes from doing, making, and moving out into the world. Play, adventure, creating, being moved by art. It's the rarest, in about one in four moments.

  • Creativity and Expression

    Using art, music, movement, or any form of expression to bring what lives inside you into the world, and finding in that expression a profound sense of wholeness, identity, and aliveness.

    Values inside itPassion · Inspiration · Authenticity · Catharsis · Flow · Vulnerability

  • Art and Music

    The profound emotional impact of encountering and receiving art in any form (music, painting, theatre, or dance) that moves you deeply, connects you to something universal, or illuminates a truth about who you are.

    Values inside itEmotion · Connection · Beauty · Joy · Awareness · Movement

  • Adventure and Novelty

    Stepping outside the boundaries of your ordinary life, into the unfamiliar, the risky, or the unknown, and returning with a permanently expanded sense of yourself and what the world holds.

    Values inside itExploration · Curiosity · Courage · Freedom · Openness

  • Joy and Play

    The simple, lighthearted moments of laughter, play, spontaneity, and pure presence that remind you what it feels like to be fully alive.

    Values inside itSpontaneity · Freedom · Presence · Humor · Silliness

  • Celebration

    Marking milestones, transitions, or shared joy through ritual or gathering, creating memory and affirming what matters.

    Values inside itJoy · Gratitude · Connection · Togetherness · Memory
