No. 1 · The six archetypes

  • The Seeker

    Becoming

    You find meaning when you come through something and discover who you are.

  • The Anchor

    Bonds

    You find meaning through the people you are bound to, including the ones you have lost.

  • The Connector

    Togetherness

    You find meaning in being with people: friendship, play, kindness, celebration.

  • The Guide

    Contribution

    You find meaning when you give something outward: teaching, serving, building, making.

  • The Witness

    Perspective

    You find meaning in a shift in how you see: gratitude, awe, time, where you come from.

  • The Free Spirit

    Transcendence

    You find meaning when you step beyond yourself: faith, the unknown, freedom.

Most people are mostly one, with some of another. No archetype is better than another.
