Privacy

what we collect, and who can see it.

This page says what we collect, why, and who can see it. It is written to be read.

Who we are

life is meaningful is run by Bennett Daneshrad, an individual based in New York, doing business as life is meaningful. When this page says "we", it means Bennett. You can reach him at bennett@lifeismeaningful.org.

What we collect

  • Your first name and email address, when you start the free archetype, buy a Meaning Map, join the letter, or apply to share a moment.
  • The moments you write or speak for the free archetype or the Meaning Map.
  • What you send in the application form: where you live, your Instagram if you give it, and the moment you describe.
  • Payment details go to our payment provider. We never see or store your card number.
  • Basic information about visits to the site, such as which pages are opened. The analytics we use set no cookies and build no personal profile of you. Our payment provider sets its own cookies at checkout to prevent fraud.

How we use it

  • To write your archetype and your Meaning Map, and to send them to you.
  • To send you the life is meaningful letter. Starting the free archetype signs you up. Every letter has a link to leave.
  • To reply to you, and to get in touch about filming if you applied.
  • To study what makes moments meaningful, and to improve what we make.

Who can read your moments

Bennett, and the AI that writes your archetype or your Meaning Map. If you speak your moments, a transcription service turns your voice into text first.

Your moments are stored under a random ID, separately from your name, email and payment details. They are encrypted where they are stored and when they are sent.

Your moments and the archive

The moments you write for the free archetype or the Meaning Map are added to the life is meaningful archive without your name. In the archive they can be used in two ways:

  • Research. They are counted in our findings, such as how often each Meaning Driver appears.
  • Shown to other people, only if you say yes. When you write a moment, you can tick a box to let it be shown to others without your name, for example as a moment like theirs in another person's Meaning Map, or on this site. If you do not tick it, the moment is used for research only. You can change your mind later by emailing us.

Before a moment is shown to anyone else, an AI removes the names of other people in it. AI can miss a name, so Bennett checks each moment himself before it is shown.

If you do not want a moment kept in the archive, email us and we will remove it within 30 days.

How AI is used

Your archetype and your Meaning Map are written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Your moments are sent to Anthropic so the model can read them. Anthropic does not use them to train its AI, and deletes them within 30 days. Anything its safety systems flag can be kept for up to 2 years.

Services we use

We use other companies to run the site. They handle your information only to provide their service to us.

  • Stripe: payments.
  • Supabase: the database and accounts.
  • Anthropic (Claude): reads your moments and writes your archetype and your Meaning Map.
  • OpenAI: makes the image in your Meaning Map.
  • Resend: sign-in and delivery emails.
  • Vercel: hosts the site.
  • Substack: the letter.
  • Notion: holds the archive.
  • A voice transcription service, if you speak your moments.
  • A community platform, if you join the community.

Filmed interviews

Interviews are filmed only with consent, which is asked for on camera. If you were filmed and want your video taken down, email us and we will remove it from our own channels.

How long we keep things

We keep your account and your Meaning Map until you ask us to delete them. Moments in the archive are kept without your name until you ask us to remove them. When you ask, we delete your account, your moments and their archive entries. Backups, and the copies held by our AI provider, clear within about 30 days.

Your choices

  • Ask us to correct it or delete it, including your moments.
  • Leave the letter at any time, using the link in any letter.

Email bennett@lifeismeaningful.org and we will do it.
